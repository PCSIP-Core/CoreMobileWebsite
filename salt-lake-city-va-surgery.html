VA Salt Lake City: scaling digital surgical workflow

At the VA Salt Lake City Healthcare System, Core Mobile deployed its platform to streamline surgery scheduling, provider workflows, patient engagement and resource coordination in a large, complex federal healthcare environment.

The deployment

Visibility into providers, rooms, equipment and resources

Core Mobile’s CoreyHealth™ and CoreyAnalytics™ solutions helped clinical teams manage surgical scheduling with greater visibility into provider availability, rooms, equipment and other resources. The platform provided near-real-time patient information from existing EHR sources, automated patient reminders, patient-facing engagement tools and predictive analytics to support operational planning.

The surgical environment serves more than 19,000 active patients and is supported by more than 500 healthcare providers. Core Mobile also supported patient pathway visibility, EHR and lab integration, customizable forms, clinical communications, large-screen displays and analytics.
