UCLA Endoscopy Center: turning clinical workflow data into actionable insights

At the UCLA Endoscopy Center, Core Mobile demonstrated how mobile workflow technology and analytics can help clinical teams capture procedural data more efficiently, understand patient recovery patterns, and turn operational data into actionable insight.

The work

From paper charting to digital research workflow

Core Mobile’s CoreyHealth™ and CoreyWebApp™ applications supported the transition from traditional paper-based data collection to a digital workflow for tracking patient recovery and utilization. By making information easier to capture and analyze, the project reduced the time required to complete the study from 14 months to 4 months.

Core Mobile’s analytics also supported the evaluation of recovery times and scheduling patterns to help clinicians better understand resource utilization and patient flow. The work, led clinically by gastroenterologist Dr. Neal Kaushal, was subsequently presented through ACG.
