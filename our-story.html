Our Story

Built for Better Healthcare

Core Mobile began its work in healthcare in 2011 with a simple goal: help care teams get the right information, at the right time, without adding more complexity.

What started with secure healthcare integration has grown into a platform that connects clinical systems, streamlines workflows, improves patient engagement, and helps healthcare organizations make better use of their data.

01

Security at the Core

Our founder, Chandra Tekwani, brought decades of experience in networking, mobility, cloud, and cybersecurity to healthcare technology.

That background shaped Core Mobile from the beginning. Security, privacy, and interoperability were not added later—they were built into the platform from day one.

02

From Connected Care to AI

Over the years, Core Mobile expanded from system integration and workflow management into telehealth, analytics, patient engagement, clinical documentation, and real-time care coordination.

Today, that foundation powers our AI capabilities, including ambient documentation, clinical information extraction, EHR abstraction, predictive analytics, and intelligent workflow support.
