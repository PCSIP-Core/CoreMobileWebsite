How it works

From referral to completed care

01

Refer

The hospital initiates the referral with the required clinical and administrative information.

02

Coordinate

PCSIP supports authorization, provider selection, and scheduling.

03

Deliver Care

04

Close the Loop

Encounter information returns to the hospital for follow-up, continuity of care, and downstream workflows.
