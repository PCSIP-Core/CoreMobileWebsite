Features

Eyes on every room, without a sitter in every room

Continuous AI Monitoring

Video AI continuously monitors inpatient rooms, detecting movement, behavior patterns, and risk indicators in real time.

Sentinel-Event Detection

Unassisted bed exits, falls, elopement, distress, and prolonged inactivity are flagged the moment they begin.

Real-Time Alerts

Alerts route instantly to nursing stations and mobile devices, so staff intervene before an event becomes an incident.

AI-Assisted E-Sitters

One remote observer supervises many rooms at once, with AI prioritizing which feeds need attention — replacing costly 1:1 sitters.

Two-Way Communication

Observers speak directly with patients to redirect, reassure, or verify — and escalate to on-site staff when needed.

EHR & Workflow Integration

Events and observations flow into the EHR and care-team workflows — documented, auditable, and connected to the patient record.
