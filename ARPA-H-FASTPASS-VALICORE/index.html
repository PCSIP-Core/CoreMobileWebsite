Detect. Validate. Decide. Protect.

Four steps, one outcome: a bad lot is stopped before it reaches a patient. Each step has named organizations accountable for it.

Detect
Five modalities through unopened packaging

Raman, hyperspectral, X-ray, terahertz and NQR on one shared protocol, separating the medicine’s signal from the package around it.

Accountable: Valisure, ImagoAI, Middleton Spectral Vision, Quadridox, Stony Brook, Bagtronics
Validate
Truth you can defend to a regulator

ISO 17025 destructive analysis, authentic samples with chain of custody, LIU defect-controlled batches and control specimens establish what the right answer actually is.

Accountable: Valisure, LIU Pharmacy
Decide
Calibrated confidence, not a raw score

Packaging-aware fusion produces evidence; a policy layer converts it into PASS, REVIEW or SUSPECT, with abstention when the evidence is thin.

Accountable: CoreMobile AI, Netrias
Protect
At the dock, not in a data center

Screening runs at ports, warehouses and dispensing sites with a complete audit trail, so a bad lot is stopped before it reaches a patient.

Accountable: Blu Zone Bio, Johns Hopkins, Ohio State

The AI layer produces evidence. A calibrated policy makes the call.

CoreMobile AI’s TA2 pipeline, PCSIP-Q, keeps the model’s prediction and the final decision separate. A raw classifier probability never becomes an operational decision, and REVIEW is a designed route to human or reference-lab adjudication, not a failure state.

  1. 01
    Ingest and normalize
    Netrias harmonizes metadata to accepted ontologies, runs data QC and rules on what can be combined.
  2. 02
    Separate packaging effects
    Learned separation of medicine signal from container, label, foil and instrument variation.
  3. 03
    Fuse sensing modalities
    Cross-modal signatures from two or more modalities in one shared representation.
  4. 04
    Predict quality, flag anomalies
    Quality and anomaly models run in parallel, with out-of-distribution indicators.
  5. 05

    Calibrated confidence drives the policy; thin evidence routes to REVIEW.

Four research problems, each with a Phase 1 target

Beyond state-of-the-art R&D, not an integration exercise.

Lane 1
Packaging-aware signal separation

Learned separation of container, label, foil and blister contributions from drug signal, with physics-informed priors instead of per-package recalibration.

Target: usable spectra through packaging the model was never calibrated on.
Lane 2
Cross-sensor generalization

A shared latent representation across all five modalities, so models transfer between instruments, units and operators without retraining.

Target: transfer to a new instrument with limited local data.
Lane 3
Unseen-defect detection

Open-set and anomaly methods that flag counterfeit, degraded, substituted and contaminated product outside the training distribution.

Target: detect defect classes absent from the training set.
Lane 4
Uncertainty-calibrated decisions

Calibrated confidence with explicit abstention, so operator review load and missed-detection risk are both bounded and auditable.

Target: decision thresholds tied to measured, validated confidence.

54 months, gated at every phase

TA1 and TA2 integrate from the start: modes, signal processing, AI decision making and engineering advance together, with a technical down-select at month 18.

  1. Phase 1, months 1–18
    Technical landscaping

    Each mode trained on control samples to establish capability. Data harmonization, ML algorithms for centralized and edge AI, and multimodal simulators on existing PCSIP infrastructure.

    Milestone: individual modes show proof of principle on key control samples.

  2. Phase 2, months 19–36
    Down-select and multimodal integration

    The most complementary modes advance. Signal optimization between modes, enhanced secure multimodal AI, and Physical AI for integration and autonomous calibration.

    Milestone: demonstrated improvement from at least two modes.

  3. Phase 3, months 37–54
    Optimization and deployment

    Detection optimized as an integrated system; PCSIP-Q gains autonomous sensor calibration for port and warehouse conditions; final prototype validated in the field.

    Milestone: 3–5 multimodal case studies, at least one real-world case tied to ground truth.

Validation is randomized, coded and blinded
Pre-registered criteria, lot-level train/test separation and independent ground truth, across a four-stage program: feasibility, integration, robustness and operations.
