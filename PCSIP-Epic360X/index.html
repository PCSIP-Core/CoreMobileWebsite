Before the request

Steps 1–5 produce everything the 360X request carries: consult, chosen provider, AI history summary, medical-necessity decision and prior authorization number.

During care

Epic's accept, appointment and no-show messages update the CPRS consult and trigger Veteran notices through MyHealtheVet.

Closing the loop

The outcome consult note is filed and summarized in CPRS, the consult is completed and HIMS is alerted to start claims review.

Request for Services (9A) has no 360X transaction: it stays on the existing channel, and approved services go out as a new linked 360X referral.
