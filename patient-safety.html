Features

Every room watched, every incident documented

Fall Detection & Alerting

AI-enhanced camera feeds and motion sensors detect falls automatically — care teams are alerted instantly, and the incident is logged with full video context for quality and risk management.

Safe Zones & Exit Alerts

Configure safe zones for vulnerable patients — geriatric, behavioral health, or neonatal — with alerts on wandering or unauthorized attempts to leave secured areas.

Sentinel-Event Monitoring & Reporting

Combined RTLS, ambient AI, and video inputs identify and classify sentinel events — generating auto-filled incident reports with location, staff present, timeline, and transcription of recorded verbal interaction.

RTLS Wristband Tracking

Patient wristbands provide real-time location awareness across all hospital zones — fast response, workflow visibility, and automatic logging of proximity events during incidents.

Emergency Alert Buttons

Call buttons in patient rooms, hallways, and at nursing stations — plus wearable one-touch buttons that alert designated responders with real-time location data.

Integrated Communication & Escalation

Alerts tie into existing hospital communication systems — pagers, overhead, secure messaging — with automated escalation protocols based on incident severity and location.
