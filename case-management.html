Features

Structure, automation, and accountability at every stage of care

Centralized Patient Tracking

Real-time dashboards showing admission status, care progress, discharge readiness, and next steps for every patient under management.

AI-Powered Risk Stratification

Predictive models identify patients at high risk for readmission, non-compliance, mental health concerns, or post-discharge complications.

Secure Messaging & Task Management

Assign tasks, share updates, and communicate securely across the care team — including remote or community-based staff.

One-Touch Documentation

Capture assessments, status updates, and care plans with pre-filled forms and automated data population — on mobile and desktop.

Referral & Resource Coordination

Coordinate social services, community care, rehab, behavioral health, or home health — warm handoffs and fast follow-through.

Voice-to-Text & Ambient Note Capture

Dictate or passively record notes during rounds or consults — automatically transcribed, structured, and linked to the patient record.

Patient & Family Engagement Tools

Send updates, check-in requests, or discharge instructions directly to patients and caregivers via secure messaging or app notifications.

EHR & System Integration

Push and pull data from all major EHRs and clinical systems — AI-generated insights are immediately available where they're needed.

Configurable Workflows

Customize workflows to your case management protocols — acute, chronic, post-acute, or behavioral.
