One clinical AI engine, four ways it goes to work

01

Ambient AI

Hands-free clinical documentation. Ambient AI listens during provider–patient conversations — in person, at the bedside, or over telehealth — and converts them into structured, signed notes written directly into the EHR.

Real-time voice-to-text transcription
AI-driven care pathways, orders & note generation
Personalized LLM per department and user

PCSIP

The Patient Care Services Integration Platform is an AI-powered care coordination and patient engagement platform, connecting seamlessly with EHRs and clinical workflows.

Automated EHR data extraction & abstraction
Integrates with Epic, Cerner, Meditech & VistA CPRS
Built for protected healthcare networks
Explore PCSIP
03

Care Quality Reporting

Quality metrics reporting by EHR abstraction. The platform abstracts the data your quality programs need directly from the record and assembles reporting automatically — no manual chart review, no retrospective scramble.

Automated abstraction of quality metrics
Registry & regulatory reporting without chart review
Research-grade structured data capture
Explore Quality Reporting
04

Case Management

AI-powered discharge planning and insurance authorization. Patients are added to the case manager's list from EHR criteria; the platform generates discharge planning and medical necessity notes, submitted automatically to payors.

Auto-generated discharge & medical necessity notes
Faster insurance authorization, fewer delays in care
Ambient AI captures care-team conversations
