Patient Care Services Integration Platform

Powered by Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

PCSIP is an AI-powered care coordination and patient engagement platform, connecting seamlessly with EHRs and clinical workflows. It brings together diverse aspects of the patient record — EMRs/EHRs, PACS, AI/LLMs, medical devices, and wearables — enhancing efficiency before, during, and after care.
