Supporting the future of radiation oncology across VA

Core Mobile’s work with Veterans Affairs has expanded from facility-level systems integration into specialized oncology workflows — helping create a more connected foundation for radiation oncology care.

Building on Albany VA

Extending PCSIP™ into specialty oncology workflows

Building on the systems integration work at Albany VA, Core Mobile extended PCSIP™ into radiation oncology workflows, connecting clinical information, workflow management and specialty-care systems — with integration across radiation oncology, VistA/CPRS, imaging and related clinical systems.

The goal is straightforward: reduce fragmentation between the systems clinicians use and give care teams a more consistent view of the patient, workflow and treatment context.
