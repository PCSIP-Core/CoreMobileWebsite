Features

Everyday clinical activity, turned into structured insight

AI Video Monitoring for Patient Safety

Continuously monitors inpatient rooms to detect unassisted bed exits, patient distress, or inactivity — alerting nursing stations in real time.

Ambient AI Charting

Captures clinical conversations during rounds, assessments, and bedside visits — structured into SOAP notes, vitals summaries, or task updates for review and sign-off.

Rounding Notes & Handoff Summaries

Templated summaries generated from spoken input eliminate duplicative note-taking during handoffs and interdisciplinary rounds.

Integrated EHR Documentation

Structured, context-specific notes flow directly into Epic, Cerner, Meditech, and other EHRs — reducing lag and improving continuity.

Multimodal Input Capture

Voice, video, and contextual EHR data combine into the most accurate, complete picture of inpatient care events and needs.

Privacy-First, Protocol-Ready

Local video processing, audit-ready logging, and customization to facility protocols and department preferences.
