Customer Story · Surgery

2,000+ surgical cases. 200+ users. One connected workflow.

At the Washington DC VA Medical Center, Core Mobile supported the modernization of surgical workflows by giving care teams mobile and web-based tools for coordinating patient care, accessing relevant clinical information and improving visibility across the perioperative journey.

Digital coordination for surgical care

Patient context, workflow status and communication at the point of care

Rather than forcing users to navigate separate systems and manual handoffs, Core Mobile’s approach brings patient context, workflow status, clinical communication and operational information together at the point of care.

Core Mobile’s broader documentation identifies Washington DC VAMC as one of the VA environments where its technology underwent extensive clinical evaluation and surgical workflow work.
