Patient-reported outcomes in industry-standard instruments — PROMIS, VR12, SF36, MME, and more — with concerning responses flagged to staff automatically.

Personal Plan & Content Library

Prep instructions, education material, and a personal care plan tailored to each patient's procedure and progress.
