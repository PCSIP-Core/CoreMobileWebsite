How it works

From sanitization event to unit-level insight

01

Sense

IoT sensors on dispensers and sinks record every sanitization event at the point of care.

02

Associate

RTLS badges tie each event to the right caregiver and room — entries, exits, and patient contacts are logged automatically.

03

Remind

A missed opportunity triggers a discreet, real-time reminder — before patient contact, not in next month's report.

04

Analyze

Infection prevention teams see compliance by unit, shift, and role — trends, outliers, and progress against targets.

Why it matters

Measure every opportunity, not a sample

Manual observation captures a fraction of hand-hygiene opportunities and changes behavior only while the observer is watching. Continuous IoT monitoring measures every event, reinforces the habit in the moment, and gives infection prevention a true baseline to improve from — part of reducing hospital-acquired infections.
