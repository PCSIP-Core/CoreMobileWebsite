About Success Stories VA to Community Care Coordination
Innovation / Demonstration

Care should continue when the Veteran leaves the VA facility. So should the information.

Veterans often receive care across VA facilities and community providers operating different EHRs and different workflows. PCSIP is designed to help bridge that divide by coordinating referrals, authorizations, scheduling and clinical information across VA and community-care environments.

The demonstrated workflow

From point-of-care referral to bidirectional return of documentation

Core Mobile has demonstrated PCSIP workflows in which a VA care team initiates a community referral from the point of care, identifies appropriate provider availability and packages the relevant authorization and clinical context for the receiving provider. After the community encounter, PCSIP supports bidirectional return of clinical documentation and updates to VA systems.

PCSIP’s interoperability layer is designed to work with community EHRs while allowing community providers to continue working in their existing clinical systems.
