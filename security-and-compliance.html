AI observability

AI you can monitor, audit, and validate

In regulated industries, visibility into AI and machine learning systems is essential — for operational oversight, trust, compliance obligations, and secure, auditable outcomes. PCSIP™ integrates observability as a core capability: full traceability of AI and LLM operations, with real-time insight into every AI-generated action across ambient dictation, decision support, and intelligent automation.

OpenTelemetry (OTel)

Vendor-neutral, fully self-hostable tracing of LLM usage — inputs, outputs, latency, cost, and token consumption — integrating with Splunk, Grafana, and Datadog with no external SaaS dependency.

LangSmith

Deep insight into AI toolchains with token-level traces and prompt-response evaluation — deployable in private cloud or on-premises, with built-in PII redaction and audit-friendly version tracking.

Weights & Biases (W&B Weave)

Observability for LLM and generative AI pipelines — HIPAA-compliant cloud deployments with audit logging, secure BAAs, and optional export to OpenTelemetry-compatible systems.
