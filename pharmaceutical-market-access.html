One Connected Platform

CoreMobile AI is designed to work with the healthcare ecosystem already in place.

Our platform can connect with EHRs, payer systems, specialty pharmacies, patient support hubs, CRM and case-management platforms, digital health applications, and other data sources—creating a more continuous view of the patient journey.

AI and analytics help transform those signals into actionable insights, while configurable workflows allow programs to adapt across therapeutic areas, patient populations, and market access strategies.

Built to Extend Your Existing Programs

CoreMobile AI does not require pharmaceutical organizations to replace existing patient services or market access infrastructure.

The platform can extend existing programs with:

Patient and provider engagement
Access and authorization workflows
Personalized education and support
Medication adherence and refill support
AI-driven risk and engagement signals
Patient-reported outcomes
Real-world engagement analytics
Configurable escalation to patient services or care teams

Deploy as a standalone experience or integrate through APIs, SDKs, and standards-based healthcare interfaces.
