Features

One live map of patients, equipment, and staff

Patient Flow Tracking

Providers always know where patients are physically located — and where they are in the care journey — from check-in to discharge.

Asset Tracking & Utilization

Locate high-value equipment instantly, reduce misplacements and theft, limit equipment errors, and cut unnecessary rentals and searches.

Wristbands & Badges

Wristbands for patients and badges for staff provide location awareness across all hospital zones — enabling fast response and workflow visibility.

Attendance & Payroll Automation

RTLS-based attendance eliminates manual check-ins — accurate records flow straight into payroll updates.

Proximity-Event Logging

Proximity events are logged automatically during incidents — an auditable record of who was where, and when.

Infection-Risk Reduction

Accurate, real-time information about patient flow, staff, and equipment supports patient-centric care and helps reduce hospital-acquired infections.
