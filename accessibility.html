Accessibility at CoreMobile AI

We want our website and products to be usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. Many of our customers are federal agencies and health systems that buy technology under Section 508 of the Rehabilitation Act, and we treat accessibility as a product requirement.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Our standard

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as our target for this website and our products. WCAG 2.1 AA is the standard referenced by the Revised Section 508 Standards for federal information and communication technology.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As we add pages and features, we review them against this standard and fix issues we find.

On this website

Large, readable text

No text on the site is smaller than 14 points, and body copy is set in black for strong contrast.

Keyboard access

Menus, links, forms and site search can be reached with a keyboard. Press Ctrl+K or Cmd+K to search from any page.

Reflows on any screen

Pages adapt to phones, tablets and browser zoom without content being cut off.

Text alternatives

Images that carry meaning have descriptive alternative text for screen readers.

VPAT and Accessibility Conformance Reports

We provide Accessibility Conformance Reports, based on the ITI VPAT® template, for our products. Each report documents how a product supports Section 508, WCAG 2.1 and EN 301 549 requirements.

Procurement and compliance teams can request the current report for any CoreMobile AI product.
