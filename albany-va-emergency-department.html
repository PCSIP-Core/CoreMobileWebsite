About Use Cases Albany VA Medical Center · Emergency Department
Customer Story · Independently Evaluated

5–20% improvement in operational efficiency

The Albany Samuel S. Stratton VA Medical Center selected Core Mobile for a Systems Integration Program focused initially on emergency care. The goal was to connect clinical and operational systems, increase visibility into patient flow and help care teams access relevant information without relying on disconnected workflows.

The deployment

From emergency care to radiology, inpatient and radiation oncology

Core Mobile deployed provider-facing mobile and web applications, patient dashboards, integration with existing clinical systems, real-time workflow visibility and analytics. The platform was subsequently extended into additional areas including radiology, inpatient workflows and radiation oncology.

Independently evaluated results

Third-party evidence, front and center

An independent third-party evaluation reported an estimated 5% to 20% improvement in operational efficiency during the deployment. The implementation engaged more than 100 staff users and supported integration of clinical and operational information across the facility.
