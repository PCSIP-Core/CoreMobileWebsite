Referral & coordination workflow

Seamless referral, coordination, records exchange, and claims

Eleven steps from the referral order in the VA EHR to payment of the community provider.

1
Referral created in VA EHR

A VA care provider requests a community care consult in CPRS (VistA) or Oracle Health (Cerner). This triggers the PCSIP workflow.

Referral order
2
PCSIP displays community care schedules

VA staff see all available community care schedules for the type of referral requested. Once scheduling is complete, PCSIP moves the consult to the next step.

Available appointments
3
PCSIP generates past medical history

PCSIP compiles a comprehensive past medical history summary for medical necessity and prior authorization by VA Community Care.

Past medical history summary
4
VA Community Care reviews for approval

PCSIP enables full automation of VA Community Care approval or disapproval based on medical necessity.

Medical necessity determination
Exception: when medical necessity guidelines are not met
If medical necessity is met in step 4, continue to step 5.
4A
Sent to VA’s RCT team

The Referral Coordination & Tracking team receives the referral for manual review.

4B
RCT manual review

RCT reviews clinical information, guidelines, and supporting documents.

4C
Approved

Returned to PCSIP for continued processing.

4D
Not approved

Referral denied and the provider is notified with the reason, and may submit a new or updated referral.

5
VA Community Care provides prior authorization

PCSIP automates generation of the prior authorization number by VA Community Care, triggering the next step.

Prior authorization number
6
Clinical package sent to the community provider’s EHR

The referral, history, medical necessity, and prior authorization go electronically to Epic, Cerner, or Meditech through the PCSIP exchange.

Clinical package delivered
7
PCSIP informs the Veteran and manages the appointment

The Veteran is told about the appointment and preparation, and can request changes or alternate times, with a potential interface through My HealtheVet.

Patient engagement
8
Community provider delivers care; records return to VA

PCSIP collects the healthcare data generated by the community care encounter and returns it to the VA EHR.

9
Data deposited into the Veteran’s VistA/CPRS record

9A — Data is available to VA staff in VistA/CPRS the same day the community care episode concludes, and PCSIP alerts the ordering provider. With no RFS, the Veteran gets an RTC with the ordering provider.

9B — If an RFS is requested, the DOA reviews it and either adds the services to the original authorization or denies it and closes the consult.

RFS / RTC order
10
PCSIP alerts HIMS that the consult is complete

Review of codes and claims begins with the full package.

Claims initiated
11
Community provider gets paid

About 80% of community care providers are paid the next day after claim adjudication by Optum. The remaining 20%, which need manual review, are paid within a week.

80% next day · 20% within 7 days

Dental care referral & coordination workflow

Seamless referral, coordination, records exchange & claims for timely community dental care

Fifteen steps from the dental referral to payment. Dental care adds a treatment-plan loop: the community dentist proposes a plan, PCSIP validates it, and VA approves it before treatment is authorized.

Shared with the medical workflow New for dental
1
Referral created in VA EHR — dental

A VA dental provider or community care team initiates a VA community care dental referral.

Referral order
2
PCSIP identifies community dental availability

PCSIP displays available community dentists and schedules the initial consultation and evaluation appointment.

Available appointments
3
PCSIP generates dental clinical & authorization summary

PCSIP compiles the dental and medical history, relevant imaging if any, and the authorized scope of dental services for the Veteran.

Dental history & authorized scope
4
VA Community Care / RCI initial review

RCI reviews eligibility, medical necessity, and referral completeness for the initial community care authorization.

Medical necessity determination
5
Initial authorization / consult authorization

VA authorizes the Veteran to receive an initial dental evaluation and consultation.

Consult authorization
6
PCSIP sends the dental referral package to the community dentist

PCSIP sends the clinical package — referral, history, authorized scope, and prior authorization — electronically to the community dentist through the PCSIP exchange (Epic, Cerner, Meditech).

Clinical package delivered
7
Veteran attends the initial community dental evaluation

The community dentist evaluates the Veteran and develops a proposed dental treatment plan based on the authorized scope.

Evaluation completed
8
New
Community dentist returns the proposed dental plan to VA

The dentist sends the proposed treatment plan, clinical notes, x-rays and images if applicable, and supporting documents back to PCSIP electronically.

Proposed plan received
9
New
PCSIP rules engine validates the proposed plan

The PCSIP rules engine checks the proposed plan against Veteran eligibility, the referral, the authorized scope, the prior authorization, and community care policy.

Automated eligibility & scope check
10
New
VA dental clinical review / signature

A VA dentist or dental specialist reviews the plan to confirm it is clinically appropriate and within the authorized scope. This step is a human approval.

VA clinical approval
Approved

Proceeds to treatment authorization in step 11.

Not approved

Returned for modification, clarification, or denial.

Provider notified

The provider receives the decision and next steps. After resolution, the flow continues to step 11 if approved.

11
New
VA issues treatment prior authorization

PCSIP issues the final prior authorization for the approved treatment plan and sends it back to the community dental provider.

Treatment authorization issued
12
Community dentist delivers authorized dental care

The community dentist performs the authorized dental procedures as outlined in the approved plan.

Care delivered
13
PCSIP returns dental records to the VA EHR

PCSIP collects the healthcare data generated by the community care encounter and deposits it into VistA/CPRS for VA use. The ordering provider is alerted.

13A — If an RFS is requested, the DOA reviews it and approves or denies the request.

13B — With no RFS, the Veteran gets an RTC with the ordering provider.

Records available in VistA/CPRS
14
PCSIP alerts HIMS that the consult is complete

The consult is closed and the full package is sent to HIMS. Review of codes and claims begins.

Claims initiated
15
Community provider gets paid

About 80% of community dental providers are paid the next day after claim adjudication by Optum. The remaining 20%, which need manual review, are paid within a week.

80% next day · 20% within 7 days
Technology architecture

Automated connectivity, visibility, and data transfer between VA and community

Today there is no automated connectivity between the VA Operational Network Enterprise and the community healthcare ecosystem. PCSIP adds a Data Exchange Center in the cloud, secured by VA federated identity (SSOe, SSOi). Each side initiates its connection from inside its own firewall, and data is exchanged in the same session.
