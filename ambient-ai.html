How it works

Documentation begins automatically when care is delivered

On the phone in your pocket or the watch on your wrist — capture runs quietly in the background while you stay with the patient.

01

Presence detected

Video AI detects the provider entering the room; RTLS confirms they're at the bedside.

02

Secure recording

The provider–patient conversation is captured in the background — no manual start or stop.

03

Structured note generated

Ambient AI transcribes and analyzes the dialogue in real time into a structured clinical note.

04

Review, sign, write to EHR

Providers review, edit if needed, and sign off — the note is written directly into the EHR.

Use cases

One ambient engine, four care settings

In-Person Care

Video AI, RTLS, and Ambient Dictation combine to capture and create clinical notes without interrupting care — passive documentation that begins automatically when care is delivered.

Passive documentation Real-time transcription

Telehealth

Through CoreyHealth™ and CoreyPatient™, Ambient AI listens during secure virtual visits and converts the exchange into a structured note — with automated reminders, follow-ups, and patient education built in.

Virtual visit notes Automated follow-ups

Inpatient Care

Automated, contactless capture of provider–patient encounters at the bedside — real-time speech-to-text that dramatically reduces manual documentation while maintaining clinical accuracy.

Contactless capture Bedside accuracy

Case Managers

PCSIP extracts key data from the patient record while Ambient AI listens during care-team conversations — generating discharge planning and medical necessity notes, submitted automatically to payors.

Discharge planning Faster authorization
