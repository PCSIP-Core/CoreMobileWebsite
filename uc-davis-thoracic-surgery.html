UC Davis Thoracic Surgery: technology designed around the surgical team

Core Mobile worked with clinicians at UC Davis to explore how mobile, real-time workflow technology could support complex surgical care while giving providers faster access to the information they need.

The doctor tells the story

Thoracic surgery involves complex coordination before, during and after a procedure. Core Mobile was designed around that reality: connecting clinicians to patient information, workflow status and care-team communication without forcing them to move between disconnected systems.
